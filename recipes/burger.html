Burger Recipe

Description

A classic hamburger with a juicy beef patty, fresh vegetables, and your favorite toppings.

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Onion slices
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your grill or a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into 4 patties and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Grill or cook the patties for about 4-5 minutes per side, or until done to your liking.
  4. Place a slice of cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking.
  5. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  6. Assemble the burgers by placing the patties on the bottom buns and adding your favorite toppings.
